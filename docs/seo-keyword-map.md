## 2. On-Page Content & Heading Guidelines

- **H1 Tag**: Exclusively 1 per page, matching search intent without keyword-stuffing.
- **H2 & H3 Hierarchy**: Logical subtopics addressing user search questions directly.
- **Multilingual hreflang**: Reciprocal links between `/en/...`, `/gu/...`, `/hi/...`, and `x-default`.
- **Structured Data**: JSON-LD Person, WebSite, FinancialService, Article, FAQPage, BreadcrumbList.
